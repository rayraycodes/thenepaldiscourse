02 — Why we gather

Ecosystems often work in silos.

Over more than a decade of building ecosystems in different parts of the world, our founders kept seeing the same pattern: people working toward the same future, rarely in the same room.

The question we asked
“How can we create a neutral platform for ecosystem practitioners in Nepal and the Nepali diaspora to come together for shared purposes and build trust?”
Our answer

Convene at an academic institution, for its neutrality and its spirit of inquiry.

01

Neutral ground

A university belongs to no single faction, sector or generation. Everyone arrives as a guest.

02

Inquiry first

Conversations led by questions rather than agendas, so very different practitioners can think in the open.

03

Purpose in common

Time in the same room, year after year, so that ideas can grow into collaboration across Nepal and the diaspora.

04 — The Nepal Discourse 2027

Four topics. Shared imaginaries.

This spring we come together again at Harvard to share our imaginaries for four interdisciplinary topics, each one a place where Nepal and its diaspora can do more together than apart.

01

Climate

Mountains, rivers and seasons: the natural systems every other plan for Nepal depends on.

02

Cities

The cities we come from, and the cities we pass through on our journey here.

03

Culture

Reconciling the ethos of our culture with progress, without tearing the natural and cultural fabric of our societies.

04

Capital

The money, talent and institutions that help good ideas take root.

05 — Bring your story

Everyone arrives carrying something.

A village, a city, a craft, a question. The Discourse is built from what participants bring with them. Come ready to share yours.
